feat: Add IV-aware options strategy selector with regime detection and expected move analysis

BREAKING CHANGE: Replaced static Bull Call Spread recommendations with dynamic
strategy selection based on market conditions.

## Major Features

### 1. IV-Aware Strategy Selector
- Calculates IV rank (0-100) using 52-week IV range from options chain
- Fetches current VIX level for market volatility assessment
- Dynamically selects optimal strategy based on conditions:
  * Long Call: IV rank < 35 AND VIX < 20 (buy premium in low IV)
  * Cash-Secured Put: IV rank >= 65 (sell premium in high IV)
  * Poor Man's Covered Call: IV rank 35-64 (diagonal spread)
  * Iron Condor: Range-bound regime override (ADX < 20)

### 2. Regime Detection Integration
- Classifies market regime using ADX and CTO Larsson lines:
  * TRENDING_BULLISH: ADX > 25 AND CTO bullish (yellow fill)
  * TRENDING_BEARISH: ADX > 25 AND CTO bearish (blue fill)
  * RANGE_BOUND: ADX < 20
  * TRANSITIONING: ADX 20-25
- Applies regime override rules:
  * Bearish trends: Suppresses options, shows warning
  * Range-bound + low IV: Recommends Iron Condor instead of Long Call
  * Bullish/transitioning: Uses IV-based selection

### 3. Expected Move Analysis Panel
- Calculates IV-based expected moves:
  * 1-week: price * IV * sqrt(5/252)
  * 1-month: price * IV * sqrt(21/252)
  * 45-day: price * IV * sqrt(45/252)
- Delta-to-probability translation table:
  * Shows actual strikes at 0.30, 0.20, 0.15, 0.10 delta
  * Displays probability OTM and distance from current price
- Pattern target comparison:
  * Compares pattern target to 45-day expected move
  * Warns if target exceeds expected move range
  * Suggests longer-dated options (60-90 DTE) when needed
